Training split: 1,050 rows. Test split: 350 rows. Precision 49.47%; recall 77.05%. Recall is higher than precision, so this model catches more churners but also creates false alarms.

What should you learn?

Why stratify a train/test split? Why fit scaling only on training data? What does a false negative mean in churn? How would changing the threshold affect precision and recall?

Important: these metrics are from a fixed synthetic dataset and a held-out test split. They are for learning only and should never be used to decide real customer treatment or presented as a production model result.
PROJECT 03 / RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION

DocuMind

Ask questions over a compact knowledge base. This prototype uses chunked lexical retrieval, ranks relevant passages and shows their source titles. It deliberately stays retrieval-only; no hidden LLM call is claimed.
